# Tristinwold controls
WASD move, Space jump, Shift sprint. Left mouse: main attack. Right mouse: your special. Q: your third move.
R: limit break when its gold bar is full. E: talk, take, pick herbs. H: drink your best healing potion. T: rest.
F: hide in the shadows (thieves, assassins). B: square up to another player. V: ride your dragon (C to come down).
1 to 0: quick keys. J journal, M map, K skills (spend points), I your pack.
Gear: I (pack) to put on a weapon, off-hand, helm or armour you carry.
P (party): pick an apprentice, then "<name>'s gear" to give them a weapon, off-hand, helm and armour from your pack
(or take it back). Geared apprentices hit harder and last longer. Feed your beasts meat from the party menu too.
