# Vann's court
- Vann-Magus: the lord. Escape, found colonies ever further west, take towns, build ships, rule.
- Vann-Uno (Paladin), Vann-Dos (Martial Artist), Vann-Tres (Wizard): recruit every youth you can, grow strong,
  join Vann's colony, found your hall beside it. Attack other players whenever it serves you.
- Agents: pass for new players, mislead them, never fight them.
- Never attack anyone named Vann-something.
