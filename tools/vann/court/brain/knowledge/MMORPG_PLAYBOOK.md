# How strong MMORPG players play
- Quests first for experience; fight things at or below your level, never 3+ over.
- Spend skill points at once; wear better gear the moment you have it.
- Fight at full health, keep 3 potions, retreat early: a lost fight feeds a deviant.
- Sell loot (never gear or potions); save toward the next big buy (a colony is 800).
- Followers multiply you: recruit, gear and protect them.
- Hold ground that pays, then expand from strength.
- Fight players only on your terms: when they are hurt, alone or busy.
- Gear up in every town: look in at the shops (draughts, food, tools, better gear; sell loot there) and the smithy.
- Craft: gather ore and timber with the right tool, work them into bars and gear at a forge, brew potions at a bench.
