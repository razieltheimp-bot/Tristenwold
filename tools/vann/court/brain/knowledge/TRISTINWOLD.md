# Tristinwold
- Apprentices: from level 3, any youth (11-16) follows you for 25 crowns. Paladin, Wizard, Martial Artist: no limit.
- Colony: level 5 and 800 crowns, on open dry land over ~1.2 km from any town. Its reeve grows it with timber, stone,
  iron and crowns. Each next colony further out along the frontier.
- Halls (level 5): Paladin chapel 350, Martial Artist monastery 400, Wizard spire 450.
- Councils: earn a seat, then send raiders; the last one at the table rules the town and its purse.
- Ships: sloop 2,500 at Osric Tarr's yard; an apprentice can command her past the charts.
- Greyhollow mine: the old face does nothing until Dagna Flint at the quarry sends you; otherwise climb out.
- Danger rises with distance from Tristinwold; turn back when warned. Run from a deviant that beat you.
