# Creatures

- `fox.glb`: the Fox from the Khronos glTF Sample Assets (https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Assets).
  Model by PixelMannen, CC0. Rigging and animation by tomkranis, CC-BY 4.0
  (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). glTF conversion by @AsoboStudio and @scurest, CC-BY 4.0.
  `wolf_coat.png` and `ice_wolf_coat.png` are its texture repainted by `tools/creatures/recolour_fox.py`.
- `deer.glb`: DeerArmature by Polygonal Mind, CC0, from https://github.com/ToxSam/cc0-models-Polygonal-Mind
  (catalogued in https://github.com/ToxSam/open-source-3D-assets).
